Type Has Jobs

BANGERS
Display
Hero headlines, section impact, poster CTA text, stats. Uppercase or title case, with stroke/shadow. Never for forms, legal copy, or paragraphs.
Sora
Structure
Subheadings, navigation, buttons, card titles, labels & badges. SemiBold/Bold; letter-spacing works well for labels.
Inter
Body
Body copy, forms, tables, disclaimers, legal text — anything that must read comfortably. Regular/Medium, generous line-height.
